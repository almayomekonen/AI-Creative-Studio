# Camera & Lighting Direction — TEMPO — Every Second Has a Beat

| Shot | Camera | Lens | Lighting | Intent |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S01 (ECU dial centre and red hand) | Imperceptible linear push (a clock doesn't ease) | 85 mm, shallow DOF | Soft white cyc light, specular on the crystal | Hook: the tick, extreme close. |
| S02 (Flat Swiss grid) | Locked | n/a | Flat | Scale shift: the second becomes typography. |
| S03 (Graphic frames) | Locked, rotating geometry | n/a | Flat Swiss palette | A life, one second at a time. |
| S04 (MCU hero watch) | Orbit right-to-left with a slight crane down | 50 mm | White infinity cove, soft key top-left, contact shadow | Reveal the whole object. |
| S05 (Wide, particles) | Slow orbit | 40 mm | Red glow on black — the only dark scene | The turn: in a sudden black void, the second hand melts into 'Now.' |
| S06 (Hero + lockup) | Slow orbit | 50 mm | White cove | Back to white; the hand keeps ticking under the logo. |

Camera data (orbit rad / dist or fill / height / lookY / lens mm) lives in each scene's `props.camera` in the spec; product framing uses `fitDistance` so the same shot frames correctly at any aspect.
