# Creative Concept — TEMPO — Every Second Has a Beat
> Generated with the `studio-concept-engine` method.

**Logline:** A watch ad cut entirely to the tick of its own second hand — the edit is the movement.

**Big idea:** Count / clock: precision expressed as rhythm. The music is built from ticks; every cut lands on a second.

## Routes considered
| Route | Idea | Why | Risk / verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Edit Is the Movement** | Cut every shot on the tick; build the score from ticks. | Product truth (precision) becomes the film's form; very ownable; Swiss grid identity. | Could feel cold — counter with the human montage words and one dramatic black 'Now.' moment. |
| **Time Lapse Life** | A day compressed into 20 s around the wrist. | Relatable. | Needs people/locations — not producible procedurally. Killed. |
| **Swiss Grid Cathedral** | The watch as architecture; camera flies through the movement. | Spectacle. | Requires an internal mechanism model we don't have yet — logged for the roadmap. |

**Chosen:** The Edit Is the Movement — Form = message; strongest originality; fully producible; the black 'Now.' contrast beat gives the progression a turn.

## Structure
- **Hook (first 1.5 s):** Frame 0: an extreme macro of the red second hand mid-tick, shallow focus, on a white world; VO and type say 'Every second has a beat.' Instantly legible and rhythmic.
- **Progression:** Escalation of tempo: one tick → a giant 60 → a one-word-per-second life montage → the watch revealed whole → a sudden black moment where the second hand dissolves into 'Now.' → lockup on white.
- **Ending:** Contrast + callback: from the only black scene back to white, the hand keeps ticking under the logo; final tick lands on the last frame.
