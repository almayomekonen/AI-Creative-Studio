# Camera & Lighting Direction — Social CRM — The Employee That Never Sleeps

| Shot | Camera | Lens | Lighting | Intent |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S01 (Swarm → macro push-in on a phone) | Starts inside a storm of message bubbles rushing past the lens; storm parts; aggressive 14→2 unit dolly into the phone with handheld shake and slight roll | 24 mm (wide, aggressive) → 35 mm | Dark luxury office at night: cool rim from the window city-bokeh, phone screen as the key, red notification glints | THE CHAOS — stop the scroll with density and tension. |
| S02 (Convergence → hero object → interface) | Locks to centre; slow orbit around the forming core; push into its glass face | 50 mm | Darkness collapses into emerald: the core's inner light becomes the key; chrome reflects a procedural studio | THE ARRIVAL — all messages converge into one point and the agent is born. |
| S03A (MCU glass interface slab) | Slow orbit left with push | 50 mm, DOF on the slab | Emerald guide light from the agent orb; cool rim | Conversations: a WhatsApp lead arrives; the agent answers naturally in Hebrew. |
| S03B (Document forming + owner approval in WhatsApp) | Camera travels along the emerald trail to the document, rises slightly | 50 mm | Paper catches warm-white key; emerald approval glow | Quotes: a quotation forms in 3D and is approved by the owner before sending. |
| S03C (Week calendar, one slot rises out of the grid) | Travel + slight dutch correction, settles frontal | 40 mm | Grid self-lit, slot glows emerald | Meetings: an appointment is created and confirmed. |
| S03D (Receipt strip extruding downward) | Tilt-down following the print | 50 mm | Soft top light on paper, emerald rim | Receipts: a professional receipt prints. |
| S03E (Field of customer dots → highlighted cluster + insight) | Pull back to reveal the field, then push to the cluster | 35 mm | Dim data field, highlighted cluster blooms emerald | Opportunities: business data transforms into one highlighted opportunity. |
| S04 (Phone on a desk; night becomes warm morning daylight; the owner is implied (notebook with a growth plan, coffee, an empty chair turned to the window)) | Slow, calm dolly along the desk; shallow focus | 50 mm → 85 mm | Dark cool night → warm low sun through the window, volumetric beams, dust in the light | THE PAYOFF — complexity collapses; silence; time returns. |
| S05 (Hero object → logo icon → wordmark → tagline) | Frontal, slow push | 85 mm | Chrome and emerald on black, glint sweep across the icon | BRAND REVEAL — the hero becomes the real Social CRM logo. |

Camera data (orbit rad / dist or fill / height / lookY / lens mm) lives in each scene's `props.camera` in the spec; product framing uses `fitDistance` so the same shot frames correctly at any aspect.
