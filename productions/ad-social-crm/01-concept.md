# Creative Concept — Social CRM — The Employee That Never Sleeps
> Generated with the `studio-concept-engine` method.

**Logline:** Your business never stops talking. Now someone answers — an employee made of light that already knows your business, working inside WhatsApp.

**Big idea:** The agent IS the logo: Social CRM's three-node graph becomes a living object — a liquid-chrome core in an emerald-lit glass shell, orbited by the brand's three nodes. It is born from the chaos of unanswered messages, works through five real jobs, gives the owner their time back, and collapses into the real logo.

## Routes considered
| Route | Idea | Why | Risk / verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Born From Chaos** | The agent is literally made from the flood of unanswered messages, then works through a continuous five-station journey. | Turns the owner's daily pain into the origin of the hero; one continuous world; strongest product truth; the logo-as-hero gives a perfect ending. | Five capabilities in 13 s can feel like a list — solved by making each object transform into the next (bubble → quote → calendar slot → receipt → data). |
| **Night Shift** | A clock runs through one night; the agent handles everything while the owner sleeps; morning reveal. | Very relatable. | Needs a person sleeping — not honestly producible procedurally; slower pace. Folded into the payoff's night → day light change. |
| **The Org Chart** | An org chart of a tiny business where one new box fills every role. | Clever and clear. | Diagrammatic, reads like a SaaS explainer — exactly what the brief forbids. Killed. |

**Chosen:** Born From Chaos — The only route where the visual hero, the story arc and the brand mark are the same object. Every scene is causal (the previous object becomes the next), which is what makes it feel like an elite tech commercial rather than an explainer.

## Structure
- **Hook (first 1.5 s):** Frame 0: you are already inside a storm of Hebrew WhatsApp messages rushing at the lens; pings stack into a riser; the storm parts to reveal one phone glowing in a dark office and the camera dives at it. Two hook versions were rendered (swarm-first vs push-first); see 01-concept.md for the selection.
- **Progression:** Chaos (darkness, density) → Arrival (everything converges into one point; the agent is born) → Power (one continuous camera journey through five stations: conversation → quote → meeting → receipt → opportunity, each object transforming into the next) → Payoff (complexity collapses into the phone; silence; night becomes warm morning daylight) → Brand (the agent becomes the real logo).
- **Ending:** World-is-the-product reveal: the hero object the viewer followed for 30 s resolves into the actual Social CRM icon, the wordmark types on, then the tagline 'פחות להתעסק. יותר להצמיח.' lands on the final beat.
