# Creative Concept — ÉTHER — Wear the Dream
> Generated with the `studio-concept-engine` method.

**Logline:** A fragrance is a place you can't visit — so we built the place, and put it in a bottle.

**Big idea:** Portal / journey: through the glass into a pastel dreamworld, and the dream condenses back into the name.

## Routes considered
| Route | Idea | Why | Risk / verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Place in the Bottle** | Travel through the glass into a dreamworld, then return. | Makes the abstract (scent) spatial; perfect for portal + surreal world + particle reassembly. | Dream ads are a genre — avoid clichés by keeping it architectural (colonnade, mirror sea) rather than flowers and clouds. |
| **Silk Weather** | Weather made of fabric — silk rain, satin fog. | Beautiful textures. | Hard to tie to a product truth; mostly mood. Killed. |
| **Breath Counter** | One breath in = one scene; out = transition. | Strong rhythm device. | Too cerebral for 20 s; folded into the hook line instead. |

**Chosen:** The Place in the Bottle — Strongest progression (touch → object → place → memory → product) with two unexpected transitions (through-the-glass portal, bottle-silhouette particle dissolve).

## Structure
- **Hook (first 1.5 s):** Frame 0: lavender silk in slow motion, a single soft-focus line 'Breathe in.' — tactile, calm, scroll-stopping because it's the opposite of everything loud around it.
- **Progression:** Journey: silk (touch) → the floating bottle (object) → through the glass into an endless dawn colonnade (place) → the bottle dissolves into a constellation that becomes the name (memory) → macro on the pearl stopper → lockup.
- **Ending:** Callback + reveal: the world returns into the bottle; VO 'But you can wear it.' lands as the lockup resolves — the place becomes the product.
