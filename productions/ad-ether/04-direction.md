# Camera & Lighting Direction — ÉTHER — Wear the Dream

| Shot | Camera | Lens | Lighting | Intent |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S01 (Full-frame macro of silk folds) | Locked; the fabric moves | 100 mm macro feel | Soft top-left key, satin sheen | Hook: calm tactile texture, an invitation. |
| S02 (MS floating bottle) | Slow orbit with gentle crane up | 50 mm | Lavender sky dome, warm key, violet and pink rims | Meet the object, weightless. |
| S03 (EWS colonnade over a mirror sea) | Dead-centre dolly down the corridor through every arch (vertical-native framing), sun rising | 35 mm | Pastel dawn, low sun behind the arches, mirror reflections | The place: through the glass into an endless dawn. |
| S04 (Wide, particles) | Slow orbit | 40 mm | Glowing points on deep violet night | Memory: the bottle dissolves into a constellation that writes the name. |
| S05 (CU stopper and shoulders) | Push-in with drift | 85 mm, shallow DOF | Iridescent highlights from the sky dome | Detail: the pearl stopper, iridescent. |
| S06 (Hero + lockup) | Gentle orbit | 50 mm | Dawn sky dome | The place becomes the product. |

Camera data (orbit rad / dist or fill / height / lookY / lens mm) lives in each scene's `props.camera` in the spec; product framing uses `fitDistance` so the same shot frames correctly at any aspect.
