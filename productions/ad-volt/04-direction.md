# Camera & Lighting Direction — VOLT — Crack the Night Open

| Shot | Camera | Lens | Lighting | Intent |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S01 (ECU ring-pull) | Slow push-in and drift over the lid, DOF on the tab | 85 mm macro feel | Void stage: only lime (left) and magenta (right) rims skim the aluminium from behind; no key light — the metal is drawn by its edges | Hook: mystery macro, then the crack. |
| S02 (POV forward through a tunnel) | Accelerating fly-through with increasing twist | Wide 24 mm feel | Self-lit voltage rings, core flashes on every kick | Through the opening into pure energy — escalation. |
| S03 (Wide, particles fill the frame) | Slow orbit around the particle field | 40 mm | Additive glow, bloom | Transformation: a lightning bolt melts and re-forms into the name. |
| S04 (Flat graphic frames) | Locked; shapes rotate | n/a (2D) | Flat neon palette swaps | Rhythm: claims delivered one per beat. |
| S05 (Low-angle hero, MS → MCU) | Orbit right-to-left while pushing in | 35 mm, low and heroic | Lime + magenta rims, white top key, mirrored grid floor | The product lands like a meteor — hero. |
| S06 (Logo over spinning can) | Gentle orbit | 50 mm | Neon stage | Lockup + button callback. |

Camera data (orbit rad / dist or fill / height / lookY / lens mm) lives in each scene's `props.camera` in the spec; product framing uses `fitDistance` so the same shot frames correctly at any aspect.
