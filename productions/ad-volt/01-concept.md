# Creative Concept — VOLT — Crack the Night Open
> Generated with the `studio-concept-engine` method.

**Logline:** The sound of a can opening is a portal: one crack and the night fills with voltage.

**Big idea:** Sensory translation — the CRACK becomes a doorway into pure energy.

## Routes considered
| Route | Idea | Why | Risk / verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Crack Portal** | The ring-pull crack opens a portal into a voltage tunnel; energy assembles the logo. | Turns the single most recognisable product sound into the whole film; strong hook; showcases portal/particles/neon. | Tunnel could read as generic sci-fi — keep the palette strictly acid lime + magenta and cut on beats. |
| **Night Shift Meter** | A battery meter UI drains as the night goes on; each sip recharges the world. | Clear benefit story. | UI-heavy, looks like an app ad; weaker visual spectacle. Killed. |
| **Silent Club** | A club in total silence until the can opens and sound+light floods back. | Great contrast idea. | Needs people and a real location — not producible honestly with a procedural stack. Killed. |

**Chosen:** Crack Portal — Highest hook + transition potential, 100% producible, the product truth (the jolt of opening) is the spine of every scene.

## Structure
- **Hook (first 1.5 s):** Extreme macro of the ring-pull in total darkness; on beat 2 the word CRACK. slams with sparks and a flash. Works sound-off (sparks + slam) and sound-on (the crack hit).
- **Progression:** Journey: through the opening → a voltage tunnel → energy condenses from a lightning bolt into the brand name → rhythm montage → the can lands like a meteor → logo.
- **Ending:** Button: logo lockup, then a final spark burst on the last beat as the VO says the line — a callback to the opening crack.
